Waxing care

A few simple things can help you prepare and feel more comfortable afterward. Follow any specific guidance Emily gives you.

Before your wax

  • Let hair grow to about ¼ inch so the wax can grip it.
  • Arrive with clean, dry skin and avoid applying oils or heavy lotion to the area.
  • Gently exfoliate a day or two beforehand, not immediately before your appointment.
  • Let Emily know about skin sensitivities or products you use on the area.

After your wax

  • Keep the area clean and avoid rubbing or touching it unnecessarily.
  • For the first day, avoid heat, intense sweating, and tight clothing on the waxed area.
  • Skip exfoliation until the skin feels settled, then exfoliate gently between appointments.
  • Use a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer if your skin needs it.

This is general care information, not medical advice. If you have a skin condition or are unsure whether waxing is right for you, check with a qualified healthcare professional and ask Emily before booking.

Waxing, explained

New to waxing? Here are a few straightforward answers to common questions.

What is waxing?

Waxing removes hair from the root using wax applied to the skin. Regrowth and results vary from person to person.

Does waxing hurt?

Sensation varies by person and treatment area. You may feel a brief sting or tug; let Emily know if you need a pause or have concerns.

How long should the hair be?

About ¼ inch is a useful general guide. If you’re unsure whether the hair is long enough, ask Emily before your appointment.

Can I wax if my skin is irritated or I use active skincare?

Waxing may not be suitable for irritated, broken, or sunburned skin, and some skincare products can make skin more sensitive. Share what you use with Emily and check with a healthcare professional if you’re uncertain.
